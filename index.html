How it works

From a single conflict focus to a supervisor-level brief: the pipeline turns open-source signals into one escalation readout and a bottom line.

  1. 1

    Query

    You choose a conflict or theater. That focus drives every agent run and keeps the picture comparable over time.

  2. 2

    Agents

    Parallel OSINT collectors pull FININT, GEOINT, SIGINT, news, cyber, energy, and more—each with its own score and evidence.

  3. 3

    Synthesis

    A supervisor layer weighs contradictions, merges timelines, and produces a unified threat read—so you are not juggling twelve tabs.

  4. 4

    BLUF

    Bottom line up front: escalation score, key findings, and what to watch next—briefing-style, ready to share or drill down.

Intelligence agents

Specialized collectors map to standard analytic disciplines. Each returns structured scores and source-backed lists you can inspect in the dashboard.

FININT

Financial Intelligence

Markets, prediction signals, sanctions-related flows, and financial stress around the conflict.

SIGINT

Signals Intelligence

Military aviation, maritime movement, and anomaly patterns from open tracking feeds.

GEOINT

Geospatial Intelligence

Thermal hotspots, infrastructure context, and geospatial corroboration.

SOCMINT

Social Media Intelligence

Social channels and grassroots signals—mobilization, narratives, and fast-moving chatter.

NEWS

News Intelligence

Curated wire and outlet coverage volume, tone, and breaking context.

CYBER

Cyber Threat Intelligence

Known exploits, threat reporting, and internet-wide scan context where relevant.

ENERGY

Energy & Commodities

Oil, gas storage, food and fertilizer stress—second-order effects on escalation.

PROTEST

Protest & Civil Unrest

Protests, riots, and civil unrest indicators tied to the theater.

DIPLO

Diplomatic Intelligence

Sanctions, legal fora, and diplomatic signaling from open sources.

PROXIMITY

Proximity Analyzer

Strike-to-civilian-infrastructure correlation and collateral-risk cues.

TECHINT

Technical Intelligence

Systems, transfers, and dual-use technology signals from open data.

CHOKEPOINT

Chokepoint Monitor

Strategic maritime passages—traffic stress and disruption risk.

GREYNOISE

GreyNoise Cyber Monitor

Background scanning noise vs. targeted activity for network-level context.
